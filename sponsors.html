Partnership opportunities

SKAA 2027 – the Shoulder & Knee Arthroscopy to Arthroplasty Symposium – is a two-day meeting on current concepts in shoulder and knee orthopaedics and rehabilitation, with a single room, six live Orthopaedics vs Physiotherapy battles and an international faculty. We invite companies to join us as industry partners and connect directly with orthopaedic surgeons, sports medicine physicians, physiotherapists, kinesiologists and healthcare decision-makers from Slovenia and the region.

The symposium offers partners a platform to present their technologies, demonstrate products at the exhibition stands during all breaks and engage participants in an educational setting – including side events held during the breaks.

SKAA 2027 has been submitted to Ethical MedTech for assessment of compliance with the MedTech Europe Code of Ethical Business Practice. The assessment is in progress.
