From instability to arthroplasty

Dates
18–19 May 2023
Venue
Valdoltra Orthopaedic Hospital, Ankaran
Format
6 live surgeries, lectures and “bring your case” sessions

The shoulder-only edition of the series, held at the Valdoltra Orthopaedic Hospital: shoulder arthroplasty with navigation, glenohumeral instability from soft tissue to bony procedures, periprosthetic infections and new trends in tissue healing.
