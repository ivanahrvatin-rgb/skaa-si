Programme

Scientific programme 2027

Two days, two joints, one question at every table: what is the best way back to play? Each topic opens with a state-of-the-art lecture, continues with a head-to-head battle between orthopaedic surgery and physiotherapy, and closes with a re-live surgery block paired with the matching rehabilitation. Each day ends with a poster battle. We want everyone in the room to take part, so come ready to vote for your winners.
